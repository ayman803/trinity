## [search] Idea: re-screen the parked ideas on today's engine

**What it is.** Several ideas were tested on a very different engine and
parked as "about zero":
- hist-prune (3 Oct, +0.3 +/- 4.1): pruning quiet moves by their history,
- nonpawn-corr (4 Oct, +1.0 +/- 2.7): a second correction history,
- tm-unsure (13 Oct, +3.9, not merged) and tm-plan (gate loss, +0.7 at 40/10),
- 16 king buckets as a recipe (+4.5 vs a different-seed control).

Since then the search changed in ways that alter exactly the conditions
these ideas depend on: two eval-based pruning rules are gone (+58, +60),
the tree is about 30% bigger at the same depth (bench 780879 -> about
1.0 million nodes), move ordering changed (#38, speed-pick2), and the time
use changed (tm-reserve). An idea that saved little work in a tightly
pruned tree can save much more in a bigger one; an idea that cut too much
when other rules also cut can be fine now that they don't.

Re-test the two cheapest first, on the current main, with predictions
written before: hist-prune (it prunes by move ordering, not by the static
eval, the kind of pruning this week's results favour: lmp-wide -5,
futility wider +5.8 but no-fut -7) and nonpawn-corr (the eval-bias table
shows the static eval is least reliable exactly in unbalanced, material-
down positions, which a non-pawn correction targets).

**Why it might work.** Interaction effects (factorial experiments,
ecology, medicine: a treatment that does nothing in one context can work
in another, because its effect depends on the other factors present).
The #38 split result just showed strong interaction inside one change;
the removal campaign changed the context for every older idea at once.
Re-screening after a large change of regime is cheap insurance against
having discarded good ideas for the wrong reason.

**Cheap test.** The branches still exist or are a few lines each; two
SPRTs at 8+0.08 [0, 5] on GitHub, which has room (the search lane is
below its two-a-day pace).

**Prediction.** hist-prune: -2 to +6, about 35% to pass. nonpawn-corr:
-2 to +4, about 25%. Kept against rated engines about 60% (search
changes in this campaign kept most of their gain at 40/10).

**Risk.** Two GitHub slots for perhaps nothing; low cost. Do not re-test
the network or time-management ideas the same way without a reason
specific to them (they depend less on the pruning changes).
