## [network] Idea: judge recipes by two random starts each, and keep the best start

**What it is.** If two from-scratch runs of the *same* recipe differ by
around 10 Elo (the control suggests this; its full test will tell), then
three things follow:
1. **Testing.** One run per recipe cannot tell a recipe's effect from the
   luck of its random start. KB16 (-2) vs control (about -15) could be a
   +13 bucket effect or pure luck. Rule: a recipe change needs at least
   two runs (two seeds) before a verdict, and is compared with the mean
   of two control seeds, not with main's single network.
2. **Why beating main is hard.** main's network was kept because it won
   (+80), so it is probably an above-average draw of its recipe. Every
   new run regresses toward the recipe's average and looks worse than
   main even when the recipe is equal.
3. **A cheap way to win.** Turn that luck into a gain on purpose: train
   the best recipe with 3-4 different random starts (unattended, one per
   night, once the runner may train) and keep the best, chosen on about
   2000 games each against main on GitHub. Then confirm the chosen one
   with a fresh SPRT, because the winner of a contest is again a lucky
   draw.

**Why it might work.** Statistics of order statistics and the winner's
curse (auction theory; regression to the mean in medicine): the best of
N noisy draws is above the average by about 0.56, 0.85 and 1.03 standard
deviations for N = 2, 3 and 4. In machine learning, random restarts of
stochastic training are a standard way to escape a bad start. With a
run-to-run spread of about 7-10 Elo, best of 4 is worth about +7 to +10
over an average run. That is enough to beat a lucky main by a few Elo,
at zero speed cost and no new code beyond the seed option that already
exists (TRINITY_SEED).

**Cheap test.** First let the control's SPRT run to 2000-3000 games to
measure the spread (free: GitHub, no PC). If control vs main is within
+/- 5, the spread is small and this idea is dropped. If it is around -10
or worse, train one more control seed (5 GPU hours) to get a second
point before spending more.

**Prediction.** Self-play +3 to +8 over main from best of 4 seeds of the
current recipe; kept against rated engines about 70% (it is a better
network of the same size and speed, the kind of change that has carried
over well here). Chance the spread turns out large enough (>= 7):
about 50%.

**Risk.** 4 x 5 GPU hours plus about 8000 GitHub games. The selection
step must be paid for honestly: the final SPRT has to use fresh games,
not the games used to choose.
