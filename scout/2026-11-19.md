  Control2), wdl0 needs about x1.4 (0.71 x 1.4 = 1.00). Suggested: screen

  cannot fix that, so if wdl0 loses, the shape is as likely a cause as

  margins sit mostly in the middlegame, where the shape is closest to
  the constant).
- Side note for the scale setting already in use: wdl25's best result-
