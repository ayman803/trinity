## [network] wdl-length: does training length still help at WDL 0.25?

**What the numbers say.** Main's network is long900 (900 superbatches,
WDL 0.5). Against it:
- wdl25 x1.2 (600 superbatches, WDL 0.25): +7.64 +/- 3.90 (SPRT #128)
- wdl25-900 (900 superbatches, WDL 0.25): +5.54 +/- 3.80 (SPRT #132)

Read through main as the common partner, adding 300 superbatches at WDL
0.25 was worth about -2 +/- 5.5, while at WDL 0.5 the same 300 were worth
+5.65 (long900 vs Control2) and +4.81 (900-s2 vs 600-s2). If the two
levers added, wdl25-900 should have beaten main by about the WDL effect
at 900 plus nothing else, and wdl25 (600) should have trailed it by the
length effect; instead the 600 run is ahead on paper. The indirect
comparison is too noisy to conclude, but it points to a mechanism worth
testing: both levers may fix the same thing. The result half of the
training target is one noisy sample per game; more passes average that
noise out, and a lower result weight removes it at the source. Two
remedies for one source of noise are worth less together than apart
(diminishing returns of variance reduction, the usual pattern in
statistics and signal processing).

**Test (cheap, GitHub only, both networks exist).** Head-to-head
net-wdl25-900-main vs net-wdl25x12-main (same search, same x1.2 scale:
EVAL_SCALE 120 in one, the fixed x1.2 in the other), 8+0.08, [0, 5].
One change: the length, at WDL 0.25.

**What it decides.**
1. The 1.2 network: whichever wins (or, if undecided, either; they are
   within noise of each other against main). Both have passed against
   main; picking by a direct test is better than by two indirect ones.
2. The next GPU nights. If length no longer helps at WDL 0.25, long1350
   at WDL 0.25 (two nights) is not worth running, and the next one-change
   questions are cheaper 600-superbatch runs: WDL 0.0 and WDL 0.1 (about
   5 hours each, one night, no resume needed), each against wdl25 (600).
   If length still helps, the plan stands (WDL 0.0 at 900, then 1350).

**Prediction.** wdl25-900 vs wdl25 (600): -3 to +6, about 25% to pass
[0, 5]. Kept against rated engines: not the point (diagnostic); for the
record, neither network has shown a gain over main against rated engines
yet (long900 at 10+0.1, wdl25-900 at 40/60: one night each, +/- 13).

**Risk.** Mostly that it stays undecided at the cap, which would itself
say "length adds little at WDL 0.25" and still settle the plan.
