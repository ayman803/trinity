## [search] Idea: tune the search settings where the rating is decided, against the rated opponents, overnight

**What it is.** The search settings were tuned twice by SPSA in
self-play at 5+0.05 on the UHO book (unbalanced openings). The first tune
gave +11 in self-play but only about +3 on the calibration scale; most of
it did not carry over. The runner now gives about 8 free PC hours a
night. Run the same SPSA tuner on the runner, but with the games the
rating is made of: Trinity against the four calibration opponents, the
balanced calibration book, 10+0.1 (or 20+0.2), Syzygy on. Tune only the
8-10 settings that a search change touches most (pruning margins and
reduction amounts), not all 20.

**Why it might work.** A basic rule of measurement and experiment design
(external validity): optimise under the conditions of use. Self-play
rewards exploiting Trinity's own blind spots; games against other engines
reward fixing them. The 1.3x-6x shrinkage from self-play to calibration
logged in ESTIMATES.md is the cost of tuning in the wrong place. The
runner's calibrations already score about 45% against these opponents,
which is where each game carries the most information for a tuner.

**Cheap test.** About 12,000 games a night at 10+0.1 (the 4000-game
calibration takes 2.4 hours). 30,000-40,000 games = 3 nights. Then judge
the tuned settings on the runner: tuned vs main the same night with
1.0.0 (rule 13), plus one held-out check against an opponent the tuner
never played (Inanis, Simbelmyne or Opus24 are already on the PC; any
one scoring in range will do). Pass rule fixed before: gap over main of
at least +5 on two nights, and the held-out opponent not worse.

**Prediction.** Calibration scale +3 to +8 (the first self-play tune
gave about +3 there); self-play could be 0 or even slightly negative,
which is fine here. Fraction kept against rated engines: most of it, by
construction (about 80%), minus some overfitting to four opponents.

**Risk.**
- Overfitting to four engines' quirks: the held-out opponent is the check.
- Tuning games share the PC with other night jobs: 3 nights of runner
  time instead of calibrations.
- The SPSA tool runs on GitHub today (scripts/spsa.py); it needs a runner
  job script with a results-repository mode and one approval.
