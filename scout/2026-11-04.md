## [network] Resumable training, so length can go past one night

**What.** 600 -> 900 superbatches gave +5.65. The next step on the same
line, 1350, takes about 11.3 hours at 30 s per superbatch, more than the
9 pm-6:55 am window (rule 17: about 9 h 55 min), so today it cannot run
at all. Make training resumable: every 25 superbatches save the
weights, the optimiser state and the superbatch number (the checkpoints
already hold optimiser_state/), and let a restarted run continue at that
number with the same learning-rate schedule (the cosine schedule depends
only on the superbatch number and the planned total). A stopped run then
loses at most 25 superbatches (about 12 minutes), so training becomes a
resumable job like tuning: it fits two nights, or a night plus idle
daytime, and a DISTURBED stop is no longer a lost night.

**Why it might work.** Diminishing but steady returns from more training
compute are the usual pattern in machine learning (scaling studies
outside chess find loss improving as a power of compute). The
1.5x step gave +5.65; another 1.5x would plausibly give about +2 to +5.

**Cheap test.** First the plumbing, with no GPU night spent: train 4
superbatches straight, then 2 + stop + resume 2 with the same seed and
data, and compare the two final weight files (they should match to GPU
rounding, as the drift check of 20 October did). Then long1350 (seed
20261019, same files, -Superbatches 1350), tested head-to-head against
long900 as long900 was against Control2.

**Prediction.** long1350 vs long900: self-play -2 to +7, about 30% to pass
[0, 5]. Kept against rated engines: about 70% (network gains have held up
better in calibration than search gains, e.g. the 1024 network's +80 and
+82). Disk: two extra checkpoints (about 3 GB), inside the budget.

**Risk.** Same 3.65B positions for more passes: about 25 passes at 900
already, so more length may start to memorise rather than learn; the
gain could stop. Resuming wrongly (schedule or optimiser state not
restored) would quietly change the recipe; the 4-superbatch comparison
guards against that. Needs a script change under tools/net, so it waits
for Ayman's next approval.
