## [search] Idea: reduce late moves less when the side to move is behind

**What it is.** The eval-bias table of 23 October showed that when the
static evaluation says the side to move is behind, a depth-10 search finds
much more than the static eval saw (mean +23 to +341, 95th percentile
254 to 1424 cp, rising with how far behind), while when ahead the error is
small. This week's results acted on that only through *pruning* (removing
razoring +58, removing quiescence futility +60, widening futility +5.8).
The same blind spot also affects *reductions*: late quiet moves in a node
that looks lost are searched shallower, which is where the hidden
resources (sacrifices, quiet attacking moves) sit. Make the late-move
reduction one ply smaller in nodes where the static eval is below alpha by
more than a threshold (start at 200 cp, the row where the table's error
becomes large), and leave it unchanged elsewhere.

**Why it might work.** Measured asymmetry, same reasoning as the
forecast-calibration idea of 23 October: spend effort where the
estimate is least reliable. Pruning removals have shown the effect is
real and large; reductions are the remaining place where the search
under-explores "behind" nodes. Search effort moves to a small fraction of
nodes (those far below alpha), so the cost in speed is limited.

**Cheap test.** One branch, one line of logic and one threshold.
SPRT at 8+0.08, bounds [0, 5]; if it passes, also 40/10 (the campaign's
gains held there: +51). Prediction to log first. Before games, a quick
offline check: share of nodes affected and node count change on bench; if
more than about 20% of nodes are affected, raise the threshold.

**Prediction.** Self-play +2 to +8, about 40% to pass; kept against rated
engines about 60% (the removal campaign kept most of its gain at 40/10).

**Risk.** It overlaps with the removals already made: some of the hidden
resources are now found by the un-pruned searches, so the effect may be
smaller than the table suggests. A threshold set too low turns it into a
general "reduce less" change that only costs speed.
