## Queen endings: Trinity's own games do not show a weakness

The advisor's note of 21 November and the Session's section of this
morning ("a real weakness of the network, all versions") rest on one
position from the Hobbes game, where Trinity's search says about -2.7 and
its opponent's about -0.3. I reproduced the number here (today's main:
+2.27 for Black to move before the x1.2 scale, i.e. about 2.7; with White
to move only -0.5). Then I asked what such numbers have meant in
Trinity's own games: the 150,000 held-out positions from the calibration
games contain 1,802 queen endings (one queen each, pawns only), 995 of
them with one side exactly a pawn up.

**Search verdicts, today's main (wdl25-900, EVAL_SCALE 120), depth 14,
against what then happened in the game:**

| side to move | search score | positions | won | drawn | lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a pawn DOWN | -2.5 or worse | 106 | 0% | 24% | 76% |
| a pawn DOWN | -2.5 to -1.5 | 66 | 0% | 71% | 29% |
| a pawn DOWN | -1.5 to -0.5 | 143 | 0% | 97% | 3% |
| a pawn DOWN | -0.5 to +0.5 | 232 | 2% | 97% | 1% |
| a pawn UP | -0.5 to +0.5 | 115 | 0% | 96% | 4% |
| a pawn UP | +0.5 to +1.5 | 147 | 3% | 96% | 1% |
| a pawn UP | +1.5 to +2.5 | 60 | 12% | 88% | 0% |
| a pawn UP | +2.5 or more | 86 | 78% | 22% | 0% |

- The typical queen ending a pawn down gets about -0.7 from the search
  (median) and was drawn: 375 of the 564 score between -1.5 and +0.5,
  and 97% of those were drawn. Trinity does not call these endings lost.
- When the search does say -2.5 or worse a pawn down, the game was lost
  three times in four. A -2.7 is the class of position Trinity loses,
  not a drawish one misread.
- The static network eval tells the same story (three networks:
  Control2 as 1.0.0's recipe, long900, wdl25-900 x1.2): a pawn down the
  mean is -1.3 to -1.4, the score it implies is 0.42 and the actual
  score was 0.416; with five pawns or fewer on the board the median is
  -0.8 and 82% were drawn. Rook endings as a control agree within 0.01.
- The x1.2 scale is not the cause: today's main rates these endings at
  -1.3 on average, the unscaled older networks at -1.4.
- One mild bias, the other way round from the worry: a pawn up at +1.5
  to +2.5 the search implies a score of 0.62 and the games gave 0.56
  (88% drawn). So main is slightly hopeful with the extra pawn, not
  gloomy without it.

**Reading.** On 995 of Trinity's own queen endings the search's numbers
mean what they say. The Hobbes position is one sample, judged against
another engine's units; either it is one of the 24% that hold at -2.7,
or Trinity saw correctly that it was in trouble. The game's result and a
referee decide that, not the size of the gap between two engines'
numbers. Caveat: these games are at 10+0.1; at 12 minutes + 8 seconds a
defender holds more, which this data cannot show.

## moredata600: scale and broken-run check (cloud, 15,000 held-out positions)

Result loss 0.08228 (wdl25: 0.08260); mean abs eval 214.2 cp, 1.03 of
wdl25's, so EVAL_SCALE 120 is the right scale for its test and no second
screen is needed; it differs from wdl25 by a median 29 cp (more than 60
cp in 23% of positions). Not broken. Its screen against main was -4.3
+/- about 18; the full test decides (my prediction of 20 November, -4 to
+6, was against wdl25 (600); against main, wdl25-900, the same range
applies since the two tied).
