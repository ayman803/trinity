## [network] wdl25-900: the merge candidate, one change against main's network

**What.** Main's network is long900 (900 superbatches, WDL 0.5, seed
20261019). wdl25 showed WDL 0.25 is worth about +9 at 600 superbatches
against the 600-superbatch partner. The merge candidate is the two
levers together: long900's recipe with WDL 0.25, same seed and files,
900 superbatches: `tools/net/kb16.ps1 | -KingBuckets 8 -Seed 20261019
-Superbatches 900 -Tag wdl25-900 -Wdl 0.25`. One change against long900
(the WDL), 7.5 hours, one night, no resume mode needed (the 31 October
long900 run took 451 minutes on the current approval's script).

**Why.** Against Control2, long900 is +5.65 and wdl25 x1.2 is +8.9, so
wdl25 at 600 superbatches is only about +3 +/- 5 over long900 itself: a
test of wdl25 (600) against main may well fail or stall while the recipe
is better. The two levers act on different things (how long the network
learns; what it learns to predict), so they should add, and both were
measured against the same partner. Lane order: this before long1350 (two
nights, the resume mode still unproven on the PC, expected +2 to +5) and
before WDL 0.0 at 600 (a research question, not a merge candidate).

**Test.** wdl25-900 x1.2 (or whatever scale the re-tune below settles)
vs main at 8+0.08, [0, 5]; then calibration next to 1.0.0, v2 check,
SPSA re-tune, Ayman's OK, as rule 5 requires. The free GitHub test of
wdl25 (600) x1.2 against main can run meanwhile: if it passes anyway, the
merge comes a night sooner.

**Prediction.** Self-play vs main +3 to +12; about 40% to pass [0, 5]
(the levers add if independent: +3 from WDL at this length plus the +5-6
already in main is the baseline, so the question is only whether WDL
0.25 keeps its value at 900). Kept against rated engines: about 60%
(network gains have carried over best: king buckets +17 of +19; the
1024 network +80 and +82).

**Risk.** The scale: wdl25's eval is 0.85 of Control2's and
phase-dependent (0.79 opening, 0.91 endgame); x1.2 was a guess that
beat the unscaled screen by +10 +/- 25. The re-tune has to carry the
scale (below). Also two seeds would be needed to call the WDL lever
replicated, as was done for length; the Session may want wdl25-900 at
seed 20261108 as the s2 partner (control: long900s2).
